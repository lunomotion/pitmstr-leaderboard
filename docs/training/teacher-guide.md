# Teacher / Coach Guide

For the teacher or coach signing a school up to compete. This walks you from creating an account to fielding a team at an event. No technical knowledge needed.

## What you can do

As a Teacher (School Admin) you manage your own school and its teams. You can:

- Create an account and log in.
- Register your school.
- Create and manage one or more teams.
- Add students to teams.
- Handle your charter invoice and payment.
- View your teams' results on the leaderboard.

You see and manage only your own school, not other schools.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to the site and choose sign up (`/sign-up`).
2. Sign up with your email (you will get a one-time code) or a social login.
3. Once verified, you land on your dashboard.

!!! note "Use your real school email if you can"
    It makes verification and any later role setup cleaner.

## 2. Register your school

1. From your dashboard, start school registration.
2. Enter your school details: name, state, and the division(s) you will compete in. Divisions use official names: KIDSQ, MSBBQ, HSBBQ, HSBBQ Unified, CBBQ, OBBQ.
3. Submit. Your school is now on file (this is the "charter").

!!! note "Your school may need approval"
    Depending on setup, a new school or team may need an NHSBBQA admin or your state director to confirm it before it is fully active. If your team does not appear right away, that is usually why.

For how registration and activation work behind the scenes, see [Registration & School Activation](../features/registration.md).

## 3. Create a team

1. From your dashboard, add a team under your school.
2. Pick the division and enter the team name and details.
3. Add your students to the team.

You can create more than one team if your school fields several.

## 4. Pay your charter fee

The charter fee is **$250 per team**.

1. You will receive an invoice (or find it on your dashboard).
2. To pay by card, open the pay link (`/pay/...`) and check out through Stripe. Payment is confirmed automatically.
3. If your school pays by check or purchase order, your administrator may need your vendor documents (W-9, ACH, and so on). Your NHSBBQA contact can send a Payment Package that bundles the invoice with all required documents.

See [Invoicing & Payments](../features/billing.md) for payer types and methods.

## 5. At the competition

1. When you arrive, your team checks in. A QR code is used for check-in at the event.
2. During the contest, judges score your team's turn-ins by scanning QR codes at the turn-in table. You do not score; judges do.
3. After judging, results post to the [leaderboard](../features/leaderboard.md).

## 6. See your results

1. Open the [leaderboard](../features/leaderboard.md) for the event.
2. Find your team to see its placement and scores.
3. Report cards and certificates may be available from your NHSBBQA admin after the event. See [Reports & Certificates](../features/reports.md).

## Getting help

- Can't log in: use the sign-in page's recovery, or contact your NHSBBQA admin.
- Your team or school is missing: it may be pending approval; contact your admin or state director.
- A payment question: contact your NHSBBQA billing admin.
